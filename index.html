About

About

Tea House is a cozy simulator set in a small fantasy town. In the game, you run your own tea house and experience the complete tea-making cycle — from harvesting tea leaves and other ingredients, to preparing and drying them, combining different flavors, and brewing drinks. You take care of the tea plantation, greenhouse, and garden. To unlock new recipes, you’ll need to grow new plants and purchase extras from the shop.

Details

Characters

The residents of the town are more than just customers. Each guest have their own tastes and preferences, and players need to find the tea that best suits them. Relationships with customers affect the gameplay in several ways: - understanding a customer's preferences can lead to better tips; - being especially attentive or friendly can result in unusual gifts; - conversations gradually reveal the characters' personalities, stories, and relationships with the player; - getting to know the customers gives players a deeper understanding of the small town and its inhabitants. In this way, preparing tea becomes a way for players to interact with and discover the world of Tea House.

Details

Teapots

Have you ever found yourself admired by a glass teapot, with fruits and flowers gently swirling inside and catching the sunlight? Using the tools of Unreal Engine 5, we are working on transparent materials that retain their visual lightness while fitting seamlessly into the game's stylized world. We are also paying attention to the physics of the ingredients — how they move inside the teapot, making the finished drink feel natural and beautiful.
